## Getting Started

1. **Sign up or sign in** on `/login` using any of 8 supported providers: Google, Apple, Microsoft, LinkedIn, Facebook, X (Twitter), Discord, or Instagram.
2. **Complete your profile** on the Dashboard so the competitiveness calculator and study planner can personalize recommendations.
3. **Pick a section** to start studying: PAT Academy, DAT Academy, or Interview Prep.

## PAT Academy

### Practice

1. Go to **PAT Academy → Practice**.
2. Check your **quota** — each tier has a lifetime limit on generated questions:
   - **Free:** 20 questions total
   - **Premium:** 360 questions total
   - **Premium Plus:** 1,080 questions total
3. Select one or more categories (Keyholes, TFE, Angle Ranking, Hole Punching, Cube Counting, Pattern Folding).
4. Choose a mode: **Quick Practice**, **Category Drill**, **Timed Set**, **Mixed Practice**, or **Exam Mode** (90 questions, 60 minutes).
5. Choose difficulty and number of questions.
6. Click **Start Practice**.
7. For each question:
   - Review the prompt and diagram.
   - Select an answer, then submit (keyboard shortcuts: `←`/`→` to navigate, `Space` to flag, `Enter` to submit/next, `Escape` to pause).
   - Read the L1 explanation after submitting.
   - L2 and L3 explanations are Premium-only.
   - Flag questions to review later.
8. Pause/resume with `Escape`.
9. Review results: accuracy, category breakdown, expandable question review.

### Analytics

Go to **PAT Academy → Analytics** to see:

- Overall accuracy and average time per question.
- Predicted PAT score (1–30) with confidence level.
- Accuracy by category.
- Average time by category vs. targets.
- Progress trend across recent sessions.
- Weakness heatmap (category × difficulty).
- AI-generated strengths and priority improvements.

### Generators

Interactive, procedurally generated PAT questions rendered as B&W technical SVG diagrams in the authentic recent-DAT format (Keyholes 5 choices, TFE 4 with dashed hidden lines, Angle Ranking 4 permutation choices, Hole Punching 5, Cube Counting 5, Pattern Folding 4):

- **Angle Ranking** (free for all users)
- **Keyholes**, **Top-Front-End**, **Hole Punching**, **Cube Counting**, **Pattern Folding** (Premium)

Generators create unlimited practice questions with adjustable difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard). Practice sessions use the same generators but track your quota and record attempts for analytics.

## DAT Academy

The DAT Academy covers the three sections of the Canadian DAT:

1. **Biology** — Cell biology, physiology, microbiology, anatomy, plants, evolution.
2. **Chemistry** — General chemistry only (no organic chemistry on the Canadian DAT).
3. **Reading Comprehension** — Skim-scan-deep read, keyword mapping, timing strategies.

### Practice

1. Go to **DAT Academy → Practice**.
2. Select a subject and difficulty level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Elite).
3. Answer questions one at a time with instant feedback and explanations.

### Analytics

Go to **DAT Academy → Analytics** to see:

- Overall accuracy and average time per question.
- Predicted DAT score (1–30) with confidence level.
- Accuracy by subject.
- Average time by subject vs. targets.
- Progress trend across recent sessions.
- Weakness heatmap (subject × difficulty).
- AI-generated strengths and priority improvements.

### Study Schedule Generator

Use the **Study Schedule Generator** on the DAT Academy hub to build a weekly plan based on your test date, available hours, and self-rated comfort in each subject.

### Flashcards

Flashcard deck previews are listed on the DAT Academy hub for quick review of key concepts.

## Competitiveness Calculator

Go to **Tools → Competitiveness Calculator**.

Enter:

- Current GPA and scale (4.0 or 100)
- DAT AA, PAT, and RC scores
- Province of residence
- Degree status (in progress / completed)
- CASPer quartile (0 if not taken)
- Extracurricular score (1–10)

The calculator estimates your chances at each of the 10 Canadian dental schools using a weighted scoring model. Results are sorted from highest to lowest probability and labeled **Safety**, **Competitive**, or **Reach**, with a full component breakdown.

> **Note:** This is an estimate. Admissions also consider interviews, personal statements, references, and other holistic factors.

## Interview Preparation

Go to **Guides → Dental School Interview**.

Three tabs:

1. **Guide** — Learn MMI and Panel formats, school-specific interview styles, preparation timeline, and self-assessment rubric.
2. **Question Bank** — Browse 25+ MMI and Panel questions organized by category with frequency indicators.
3. **Practice Simulator** — Run a timed mock interview with a randomized question set and model answers.

## Dashboard & Planner

### Dashboard

After signing in, visit **Dashboard** to:

- View your profile completion status and fill in details (name, province, GPA, year level, target year, degree status, undergrad school).
- See quick stats: PAT questions answered, study streak, task count.
- Jump to PAT Academy, DAT Academy, School Hub, or Planner.

### Application Planner

Go to **Dashboard → Planner** to manage application tasks and deadlines:

- **Board view**: Kanban columns (Not Started, In Progress, Under Review, Complete, Blocked).
- **Calendar view**: See tasks on a monthly calendar.
- **Task creation**: Title, category (Academic, DAT, Experience, Application, Interview, Other), priority (Critical, High, Medium, Low), due date, estimated time, school, and notes.
- **Scheduling suggestions**: 7-day lookahead with overdue and upcoming tasks.
- **Bulk actions**: Update status for multiple tasks at once.
- **Reschedule**: Move tasks to new dates with previous-date tracking.
- **Reminders**: Due-date notifications via in-app, email, and push.

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Practice daily** — Short, consistent sessions beat occasional long ones.
2. **Review explanations** — Understanding why an answer is wrong is more valuable than the score.
3. **Track your time** — PAT questions have tight time targets.
4. **Use the schedule generator** — A plan keeps you accountable.
5. **Re-take weak areas** — Use the heatmap in Analytics to target low-accuracy categories.
6. **Start interview prep early** — Three months ahead is ideal.
7. **Share your results** — The community thrives when people post their outcomes.
8. **Enable notifications** — Stay on top of deadlines with task reminders.
